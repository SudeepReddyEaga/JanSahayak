1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

In India, welfare programs are distributed across dozens of disparate central ministries and state departments. Citizens attempting to assess their welfare eligibility face severe friction due to language barriers, technical bureaucratic jargon, and complex multi-conditional eligibility thresholds.

JanSahayak automates the welfare evaluation workflow through a sequential pipeline of 6 specialized agents orchestrated with the Microsoft Agent Framework. The system provides:

  • Multilingual Voice & Text Processing: Real-time STT and speech synthesis across 12 Indian languages, with Indic numeral normalization and Urdu RTL support.
  • Hybrid BM25 + Vector RAG: Dual-tier lexical and dense trigram retrieval over 17 seeded Indian welfare programs.
  • Deterministic Strategy Engine: Zero-hallucination mathematical rule evaluation ensuring strict demographic eligibility boundary compliance.
  • GroundingGate Verification: Cryptographically verified citation tracking guaranteeing every declared benefit originates in official gazettes.
  • Language Consistency Reviewer: Quality assurance agent that verifies native vernacular syntax while ensuring official scheme names remain intact verbatim.
  • Two-Tier Redis Caching: Profile-guarded exact and semantic vector caching delivering sub-5ms latency with zero cross-profile demographic contamination.
